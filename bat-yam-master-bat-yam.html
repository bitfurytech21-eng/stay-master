Getting here

  • To get from Ben Gurion Airport to 4 Yohanan Hasandlar Street in Bat Yam, you can choose from several transportation options, including a taxi, shuttle service, or a combination of public transportation. Here are the steps for each option:
  • By taxi Taxis are readily available at Ben Gurion Airport 24/7. Follow these steps: 1. After exiting the airport terminal, follow the signs to the taxi stand. 2. Inform the taxi driver that you want to go to “4 Yohanan Hasandlar Street in Bat Yam.” 3. Make sure the taxi meter is turned on. The taxi will take you directly to your destination in Bat Yam. The journey may take approximately 30-45 minutes, depending on traffic.
  • By Private Shuttle You can pre-book a private shuttle service, such as a shared airport transfer or a private car, to take you from the airport to your destination.
  • Companies like SuperShuttle or various local providers offer these services.
  • Provide your flight details and address in advance when booking.
  • By Combination of Public Transportation You can use a combination of public transportation options, such as train and bus, to reach Bat Yam. Here’s how: 1. Take the train from Ben Gurion Airport to Tel Aviv. Follow the signs to the train station located within the airport complex. 2. Purchase a train ticket to Tel Aviv’s “HaHagana” or “Savidor Center” station. 3. From Tel Aviv, you can take a local bus or taxi to Bat Yam. 4. You can check the local bus routes that will take you closest to 4 Yohanan Hasandlar Street in Bat Yam.
  • Check-in from 03:00 pm, check-out by 11:00 am.
  • Reception and support are remote — details are sent before arrival.
