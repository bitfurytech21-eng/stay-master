master experience in Salzburg

Salzburg is a city in Austria that is famous for its Baroque architecture, winding streets, and grand palaces. It is also the birthplace of Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart. There are many things to see and do in Salzburg, including visiting Mozart's birthplace, exploring the Hohensalzburg Fortress, and strolling through the Mirabell Gardens. The city is also home to a number of family-friendly attractions, such as the Sound of Music tour and the Salzburg Zoo. Salzburg is a great place to visit for both business and leisure travellers.

master Linzergasse's serviced apartments are at the very entrance of the Alstadt (Old City), a mere 10-minute walk to Mozart's birthplace and so perfectly situated to explore the storied city. master Mirabell is just minutes away from the Mirabell Gardens, an enchanting palace with meticulously landscaped gardens. Both properties are strategically positioned near the Congress House of Salzburg, making them an ideal choice for businessmen, aside from being perfect for family vacations as well.
