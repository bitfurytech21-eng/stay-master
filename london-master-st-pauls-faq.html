General Information

  • What exactly is master St. Paul’s – hotel, aparthotel, or serviced apartments? master St. Paul’s offers Serviced Apartments that combine private living spaces with hotel-style services, including virtual guest support, housekeeping, and laundry facilities.
  • What type of traveller is master St. Paul’s best suited for – family, business, or solo? master St. Paul’s suits business travellers, couples, solo guests and families seeking spacious serviced apartments with kitchen facilities in central London.
  • Is master St. Paul’s suitable for a long-term stay of several weeks or more? Yes, fully equipped kitchens, private washer-dryers and apartment-style layouts make master St. Paul’s comfortable for extended stays.
  • Is master St. Paul’s set up for business travellers on a corporate stay in London? Yes, apartments include a table or workspace, high-speed Wi-Fi and excellent transport connections, making the property well suited for business stays in London.
  • Is there a workspace or co-working area at master St. Paul’s for remote workers? Apartments include a table or desk area suitable for remote work, together with reliable high-speed internet. There is no dedicated co-working area on-site.
  • How many serviced apartments are available at master St. Paul’s? master St. Paul’s offers 27 serviced apartments across four thoughtfully designed categories.
  • Does master St. Paul’s provide a 24-hour reception or concierge service? A concierge service is not available on-site. However, the team is happy to assist guests 24/7 via WhatsApp (+443308187011) or email (easytravelagency@trustagent.space). Guests are also welcome to use the concierge service at the nearby Leonardo Hotel.
  • How often is housekeeping provided at master St. Paul’s? Housekeeping is included; standard service with linen and towel change is provided once weekly for extended stays, with additional cleans bookable for a fee.
  • Are utilities such as Wi-Fi, electricity and water included in the nightly rate at master St. Paul’s? Yes, unlimited high-speed Wi-Fi, electricity, heating, water, and council taxes are fully included in every nightly rate without surcharges.
  • Does master St. Paul’s have a minimum length of stay requirement? No, most dates have no minimum stay; however, selected peak periods may require a two-night minimum, clearly displayed during booking.
  • Is master St. Paul’s suitable for families with children? Yes, spacious apartments, kitchens and laundry facilities make the property well-suited for families travelling with children.
  • What is the direct telephone number for master St. Paul’s? Guests can contact staff 24/7 via WhatsApp at +443308187011 or by email at easytravelagency@trustagent.space.
  • How do guests book a stay at master St. Paul’s? Guests may book directly via the website: https://www.stay-master.com/

Location & Transportation

  • Where exactly is master St. Paul’s located, and how close is it to St Paul’s Cathedral? master St. Paul’s London is located on Creed Lane, just 200 metres (a three-minute walk) west of St. Paul’s Cathedral.
  • Which London Underground stations are within walking distance of master St.Paul’s? St Paul’s Underground station is a three-minute walk away, while Mansion House and Blackfriars stations can be reached in about seven minutes.
  • How far is master St. Paul’s from London Heathrow Airport? The property is located approximately 18 miles (30 kilometres) from Heathrow, with the exact distance depending on the chosen route.
  • What is the best way to reach master St. Paul’s from Heathrow using public transport? The quickest public route is the Elizabeth Line from Heathrow to Farringdon, followed by a 15-minute walk or a one-stop Thameslink ride to the property. We recommend checking Google Maps for live updates in case of any transport changes.
  • Does master St. Paul’s arrange airport transfers for guests? No, airport transfers are not provided. Guests can use taxi apps such as Uber, Bolt, or FreeNow.
  • Is master St. Paul’s situated within the Congestion Charge zone? Yes, master St. Paul’s lies within the Congestion and Ultra Low Emission Zones; fees apply.
  • Are there grocery stores or supermarkets near master St.Paul’s? Yes, both Tesco Express and Sainsbury’s Local are located within a short radius of the property, providing easy access to everyday supplies.
  • Does master St. Paul’s provide on-site parking for guests? No, there is no on-site parking available at the property.
  • If parking is not available at master St. Paul’s, what are the nearest public car parks? Both Baynard House and NCP Thames Exchange offer parking within a ten-minute walk of master St. Paul’s. We recommend checking their respective websites for current rates and availability.

Rooms & Amenities

  • What type of accommodation is master St. Paul’s, and how does it differ from a traditional hotel? It is a collection of contemporary serviced apartments with private kitchens, living areas and self-check-in, offering independent residential comforts rather than full-service hotel facilities.
  • What apartment types are offered at master St. Paul’s? Studio, Studio with Sofa Bed, Inner Lightwell Studio and Family Apartments are offered.
  • Does master St. Paul’s provide in-room laundry facilities such as washer-dryers? Yes, all apartments are equipped with private washer-dryer units for guest use.
  • Is air-conditioning available in every unit at master St. Paul’s? Yes, individually controlled air-conditioning and heating are installed in all apartments.
  • Does master St. Paul’s provide toiletries, bed linens and towels? Yes, complimentary premium toiletries, bed linens, and towels are provided.
  • Can guests request additional housekeeping at master St. Paul’s? Yes, additional cleaning sessions can be arranged on request. Contact the team via WhatsApp (+443308187011) or email (easytravelagency@trustagent.space).
  • Are cots or extra beds available at master St. Paul’s? Yes, baby cots can be provided upon request. Extra beds for adult guests are not available.
  • Is there lift access available throughout master St. Paul’s? Yes, lifts provide step-free access throughout master St. Paul’s to every apartment floor.
  • Are accessible bathrooms available in any apartments at master St. Paul’s? Yes, a limited number of units feature roll-in showers, grab rails and lower bathroom sinks.

Food & Dining

  • Does master St. Paul’s serve breakfast on-site? Breakfast is not served on-site. Guests may enjoy breakfast at nearby cafés or join the breakfast service at the nearby Leonardo Royal Hotel for an additional charge, which is paid directly at the hotel.
  • Does master St. Paul’s have a restaurant or bar, or is it mainly self-catering? The apartments are self-catering and do not have an on-site restaurant or bar. Guests may cook in their apartment, order delivery, or visit nearby cafés, restaurants and the Leonardo Royal Hotel.
  • Does master St. Paul’s partner with local restaurants for meal delivery? No, there are no partnered restaurants offering discount codes.
  • Are kitchens at master St. Paul’s stocked with basic cooking utensils and tableware? Yes, cupboards contain pots, pans, knives, cutting boards, crockery, glassware, and cutlery, which are provided for everyday cooking.
  • Can food from local delivery apps be delivered directly to my apartment at master St. Paul’s? Yes, guests may order from popular delivery apps such as Uber Eats, Deliveroo or Just Eat. Couriers usually deliver to the main entrance, where guests can collect the order directly.

Check-in & Check-out

  • What are the standard check-in and check-out times at master St. Paul’s? Standard check-in begins at 3:00 PM, and check-out is required by 11:00 AM on departure day.
  • Can guests request early check-in at master St. Paul’s? Yes, if the apartment is ready before 3:00 PM, the staff will inform the guest to confirm an early check-in.
  • Is late check-out available at master St. Paul’s, and is there a fee? Yes, a late check-out can be arranged in advance, subject to availability and possible fees.
  • How do guests check in at master St. Paul’s? The property operates with a self-check-in system. Your unique apartment PIN code will be sent to you via WhatsApp and email at 9:00 AM on your arrival day. To ensure you receive your code on time, please confirm that your reservation is paid in full and your online registration is complete.
  • When exactly will I receive my access code for master St. Paul’s, and what should I do if it hasn’t arrived by check-in day? The apartment PIN code is sent at 9:00 AM on the day of arrival by WhatsApp and email. If it has not arrived by check-in time, please contact the support team via WhatsApp or email for 24/7 assistance.
  • Will I be required to provide ID or passport details before getting the master St. Paul’s access code? Yes, guests must complete the online check-in and provide identification before receiving their access code.
  • What is the procedure if my master St. Paul’s access code does not work when I arrive at the door? Please contact the Guest Relations Team immediately via WhatsApp (+443308187011) or email (easytravelagency@trustagent.space). A representative will verify your booking and assist.
  • How and when can I request an official invoice for my stay at master St. Paul’s? Official invoices are issued at the end of the stay. To ensure accuracy, please provide all billing or company details to our team before check-out. Unfortunately, it is not possible to change invoice details once issued.

Reservations & Policies

  • What is the cancellation policy for bookings at master St. Paul’s? Cancellation and prepayment conditions depend on the selected rate; flexible and non-refundable options are shown during booking.
  • Are pre-payment or deposit requirements in place when reserving at master St. Paul’s? No security deposit is taken, but a valid credit card is required and may be pre-authorised for any damages or incidentals.
  • Which payment methods are accepted at master St. Paul’s? All stays must be pre-paid using the secure payment link provided. We exclusively accept card payments and are unable to accept cash at the property.
  • Does master St. Paul’s offer long-stay discounts? Yes, discounted weekly and monthly rates are available for stays of seven nights or more.
  • Does master St. Paul’s allow dogs to stay in the apartments? Yes, dogs are welcome for an additional charge of 20 GBP per night. They must be trained, not left unattended, kept off sofas and beds, and must not cause any damage. Guests are responsible for any damage caused.
  • What is the maximum occupancy for each apartment type at master St. Paul’s, and are there sofa beds for extra guests? Studios and Inner Lightwell Studios accommodate up to 2 guests, the Studio with Sofa Bed accommodates up to 3 guests, Family Apartment 4 PAX accommodates up to 4 guests and Family Apartment 5 PAX accommodates up to 5 guests. Sofa beds are available only in categories that specifically include them, such as the Studio with Sofa Bed.

Attractions & Experiences

  • What are the best things to do in London when staying at master St. Paul’s? Climb St. Paul’s Cathedral, cross the Millennium Bridge to the Tate Modern, and explore Borough Market. The Thames, Globe Theatre, and Southbank are all on your doorstep.
  • Which major landmarks are within a short walk of master St. Paul’s? Nearby landmarks include St. Paul’s Cathedral, Millennium Bridge, Tate Modern, Shakespeare’s Globe and the Museum of London.
  • How long does it take to walk from master St. Paul’s to the Tate Modern? A pleasant walk across Millennium Bridge reaches Tate Modern in roughly ten minutes.
  • Is Borough Market easily reachable from master St. Paul’s? Yes, Borough Market is just across the river, approximately 1.2 km away. Most guests enjoy the 20-minute walk via the Millennium Bridge, though you can also take a quick 10-minute bus ride from nearby stops.
  • Does master St. Paul’s organise local tours or experiences for guests? We do not book tours directly, but our staff can provide recommendations for reliable local operators.
  • Are fitness centres or gyms available nearby for guests of master St.Paul’s? Yes, guests receive complimentary access to the fully equipped gym at the Leonardo Royal Hotel, just a two-minute walk from the apartments.
