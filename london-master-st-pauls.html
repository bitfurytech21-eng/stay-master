master St. Paul's

When visiting London, indisputably one of the most dynamic and multicultural cities on the planet, and an unrivaled epicenter of global finance, master St.Paul's serviced apartments offer the perfect solution, whether you're here on business or on vacation – both for short and extended stays.

Our apartments are adjacent to St. Paul's Cathedral, an architectural marvel that has graced the city's skyline for centuries. Walk over the Thames via the illuminated and innovatively designed Millenium Bridge to get to the Tate Modern, the renowned contemporary museum with works by iconic artists like Picasso, Warhol and Hockney. The fusion of history and modernity extends to the Shakespeare's Globe Theater, where you'll be transported to the Elizabethan era and experience Shakespearean plays in a faithful reconstruction of the original theater. A hop, skip and a jump – that's all it takes to reach The City, London's pulsating financial center, which magnetically draws in financial wizards, entrepreneurs and investors from every corner of the globe. master St. Paul's serviced apartments cater to business travelers, placing them strategically within reach of institutions like the Bank of England and the London Stock Exchange.

Getting here

  • To get from London airports (Heathrow, Gatwick, or Stansted) to 5 Creed Lane, which is located in the City of London, you have several transportation options. The best choice depends on which airport you are arriving at. Here’s how you can get there from each of these airports:
  • By train From Heathrow Airport (Fastest): Board the Elizabeth line to Farringdon station (approximately 30 minutes). Upon arrival, you can either enjoy a pleasant 15-minute walk to master St. Paul’s or take a one-stop Thameslink train to either City Thameslink or Blackfriars station.
  • From Gatwick Airport: A convenient Thameslink train will take you directly to either Blackfriars or City Thameslink station (journey time approximately 40 minutes).
  • From Luton Airport: The Thameslink train offers a direct service from Luton Parkway station to Blackfriars station (journey time around 45 minutes).
  • By Underground (Tube) From Heathrow Airport (Budget-Friendly): Take the Piccadilly line from Heathrow to Holborn. At Holborn, transfer to the Central line and continue to St. Paul’s station (journey time around 1 hour). Another underground alternative is to take the Piccadilly line to Earl’s Court and then change to the District line towards Blackfriars station.
  • Check-in from 03:00 pm, check-out by 11:00 am.
  • Reception and support are remote — details are sent before arrival.

Location & Transportation

Where exactly is master St. Paul’s located, and how close is it to St Paul’s Cathedral?

Which London Underground stations are within walking distance of master St.Paul’s?

St Paul’s Underground station is a three-minute walk away, while Mansion House and Blackfriars stations can be reached in about seven minutes.

What is the best way to reach master St. Paul’s from Heathrow using public transport?

Attractions & Experiences
