master La Rambla

master La Rambla puts you right on the iconic Rambla, in the very center of Barcelona's energy, color, and charm. Inside this modern 8-story building, you'll find 33 exclusive apartments designed for comfort, flexibility, and peace of mind, whether you're here for a few nights or a few weeks.

Each apartment has independent access with a secure entry code, plus an automatic video intercom and 24/7 reception service for added security and support. From floors 1 to 6, five apartments are laid out per level. The top floor, floor 8 features a one-of-a-kind penthouse with stunning 360-degree views across the city. With fast Wi-Fi, air conditioning, fully equipped kitchens, an elevator, and professional cleaning services, you'll have everything you need to feel at home, right in the heart of it all.

33 apartments

The Apartments

Each of the 33 exclusive apartments at master La Rambla is designed to feel like your own slice of the city, bright, functional, and thoughtfully laid out. The style blends modern Mediterranean charm with clean Scandinavian lines, creating a space that's as warm and welcoming as it is sleek and simple.

Whether you're here to work, rest, or explore, you'll enjoy the comfort of air conditioning, a fully equipped kitchen, and a smartly placed television for when you want to unwind.
