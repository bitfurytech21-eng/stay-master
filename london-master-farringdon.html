master Farringdon

Nestled in the vibrant Farringdon neighborhood, master Farringdon serviced apartments offers the perfect blend of historic charm and modern sophistication. With 14 thoughtfully designed family-friendly apartments, this property serves as a spacious and convenient home base for family apartments exploring London. Our generous layouts, flexible bedding options, and kid-friendly features ensure parents and children alike have room to unwind and create lasting memories.

Located on the edge of the City of London, Farringdon is steeped in history, with roots dating back to the 12th century. Today, it has evolved into one of the city's trendiest districts, celebrated for its youthful vibe and dynamic urban lifestyle. Families will appreciate the neighborhood's safe, pedestrian-friendly streets and the variety of casual cafes perfect for little ones. Situated between iconic areas like Clerkenwell, St. Paul's, and the Barbican, Farringdon puts you in the heart of London's cultural and business hubs. Enjoy a stroll along Charterhouse Street, indulge in culinary delights at renowned eateries like St. John Restaurant, or explore the lively nightlife at the legendary Fabric nightclub. Just a short walk away, families can visit the Museum of London for engaging exhibits that kids love, or let children burn off energy at nearby Spa Fields Park. From the historic Smithfield Market to world-class performances at Sadler's Wells, there's always something to discover nearby. master Farringdon combines the convenience of a central location with the comfort of a modern, private retreat. Our fully equipped kitchens make it easy to prepare breakfasts or midday snacks for children, on-site laundry facilities provide added convenience for longer stays. Be it a short getaway or an extended stay, our family apartments are the ideal base to experience the best of London. Parents can enjoy a peaceful evening in our quiet apartments, knowing kids have ample space to play and relax.
