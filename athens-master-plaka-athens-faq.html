General Information

  • What exactly is master Plaka – hotel, aparthotel, or serviced apartments? master Plaka offers serviced apartments that combine private living spaces with hotel-style conveniences, including virtual guest support, housekeeping and access to laundry facilities.
  • What type of traveller is master Plaka best suited for – family, business, or solo? master Plaka suits business travellers, couples, and families seeking spacious serviced apartments with kitchen facilities in Athens.
  • Is master Plaka set up for business travellers on a corporate stay in Athens? Yes. Apartments include workspace, high-speed Wi-Fi, and convenient transport connections for business stays in Athens.
  • Is master Plaka suitable for a long-term stay of several weeks or more? Yes. Fully equipped kitchens, laundry facilities, and apartment-style layouts make it comfortable for extended stays.
  • Does master Plaka offer complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi throughout the property? Yes, complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi covers every apartment and all shared areas for guests.
  • Does master Plaka provide fully equipped kitchens in every apartment? Yes, each apartment includes a fully equipped kitchen with stovetop, fridge, cookware, and dishwasher.
  • Does master Plaka feature an on-site laundry room for guest use? Yes, master Plaka provides an on-site laundry room with washing machines and dryers for guest convenience.
  • Does master Plaka operate a 24/7 remote service team for guest support? Yes, a 24/7 service team provides remote support.
  • Does master Plaka have an elevator that reaches all apartment levels? Yes, all floors at master Plaka are reachable by elevator, ensuring step-free access to every apartment.
  • Are dogs welcome at master Plaka? Yes, master Plaka welcomes trained dogs for an additional charge of 20 Euros per night; they must never be left unattended, stay off sofas and beds, and any damage is the guest’s responsibility.

  • How close is master Plaka to the Acropolis Hill entrance? Acropolis Hill is about a fifteen-minute walk from master Plaka.

  • Is Akropoli Metro Station within walking distance of master Plaka? Yes, Akropoli Metro Station is about a ten-minute walk from master Plaka.
  • How far is master Plaka from the centre of Athens? master Plaka is about a five-minute walk from the centre of Athens.
  • Is there any street parking available near master Plaka? Street parking in central Athens and the Plaka neighbourhood is very limited. Guests travelling by car are advised to use one of the paid public car parks nearby.

  • How often is the full apartment cleaning and linen change performed at master Plaka? Professional housekeeping and fresh linens are provided every seven days. Additional cleaning services are available upon request for a fee.
