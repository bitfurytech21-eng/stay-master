General Information

  • What exactly is master Mazeh – hotel, aparthotel, or serviced apartments? master Mazeh offers Serviced Apartments that combine private living spaces with hotel-style services, including virtual guest support, housekeeping, and laundry facilities.
  • Does master Mazeh offer complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi in all apartments and public areas? Yes, complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi is available in every apartment and all public areas.
  • Does master Mazeh feature a 24-hour fitness centre for its guests? Yes, a fully equipped fitness centre is open 24 hours a day exclusively for registered guests.
  • Is a 24-hour front desk or virtual concierge service available at master Mazeh? Although there is no staffed reception desk available around the clock, a virtual concierge team is available 24/7 to assist guests via phone calls, emails, or WhatsApp chat.
  • Is master Mazeh a smoke-free property? Yes, all apartments and interior public spaces are completely smoke-free, and smoking is prohibited inside the building.
  • Is master Mazeh suitable for families with children? Yes, the serviced apartments welcome families.
  • Are cribs or high chairs available upon request at master Mazeh? Yes, complimentary cribs or high chairs can be provided on request, subject to availability.
  • Does master Mazeh provide elevator access to the upper floors? Yes, all floors are accessible by elevator, ensuring step-free movement to every apartment.
  • What type of traveller is master Mazeh best suited for (family, business, or solo)? master Mazeh suits business travellers, couples, and families seeking spacious serviced apartments with kitchen facilities in Tel Aviv.
  • Is master Mazeh set up for business travelers on a corporate stay in Tel Aviv? Yes. Apartments include high-speed Wi-Fi and convenient transport connections for business stays in Tel Aviv.
  • Is master Mazeh suitable for a long-term stay of several weeks or more? Yes. Fully equipped kitchens, laundry facilities, and apartment-style layouts make it comfortable for extended stays.
  • Is master Mazeh more like staying in a hotel or renting a private apartment in Tel Aviv? The property offers the privacy of an apartment alongside professional hospitality standards, including digital check-in and 24/7 guest support.

Location & Transportation

  • What is the exact street address of master Mazeh? 56 Mazeh Street, Tel Aviv, 65789, Israel.
  • How long does a taxi ride from Ben Gurion Airport to master Mazeh usually take? A licensed airport taxi normally reaches the property in around 20–30 minutes, depending on traffic conditions.
  • Which train station is recommended when travelling from Ben Gurion Airport to master Mazeh? Travellers arriving by train should get off at Tel Aviv HaShalom Station. From there, you can continue to Mazeh 56 by taxi, bus, or the light rail, all of which are located by the station.
  • How long is the walk from master Mazeh to Rothschild Boulevard? Rothschild Boulevard is about 200 meters from master Mazeh, an easy three-minute walk.
  • What is the walking distance from master Mazeh to Carmel Market? Carmel Market is about a 15-minute walk from the property, which is roughly one kilometer away.
  • How long is the walk from master Mazeh to Aviv Beach? A casual 25-minute walk will get you to Aviv Beach from the property.
  • Does master Mazeh offer on-site parking, and what is the nightly rate? Yes, a secure underground parking lot is available for guests at a nightly fee of ₪60 (excluding VAT) per vehicle.
  • Can the on-site parking space at master Mazeh be reserved in advance? Parking at master Mazeh can be reserved starting one day prior to your arrival.
  • What are the recommended public-transport routes from Tel Aviv Central Bus Station to master Mazeh? Several bus lines connect Tel Aviv Central Station with master Mazeh; consult Google Maps or Moovit for real-time schedules.

Rooms & Amenities

  • Which apartment categories are available at master Mazeh, from one-bedroom to two-bedroom layouts? Apartment types include One-Bedroom, One-Bedroom with Sofa Bed, One-Bedroom with Balcony, One-Bedroom with Balcony & Sofa Bed, One-Bedroom with Large Terrace & Sofa Bed, and Two-Bedroom with Sofa Bed layouts.
  • What kitchen appliances are included in the apartments at master Mazeh? Every unit features a kitchenette with refrigerator, stovetop, microwave-oven combination and an in-room espresso machine for fresh coffee.
  • Is an in-unit washer and dryer provided in every apartment at master Mazeh? Yes, every apartment at master Mazeh includes its own washing machine and dryer.
  • Does master Mazeh supply bath amenities and fresh towels in each apartment? Yes, branded bath products and freshly laundered towels are provided and replenished as needed in all apartments.
  • Is a smart TV available in the apartments at master Mazeh for streaming services such as Netflix? Yes, the smart TV enables guests to stream Netflix or other services in every apartment.
  • Are laptop-compatible safes installed in all apartments at master Mazeh? Yes, a personal safe large enough for a laptop is installed in every unit.
  • Are iron and ironing board supplied as standard in every apartment at master Mazeh? Yes, a full-size iron and ironing board are supplied as standard equipment in each apartment.
  • Do select apartment types at master Mazeh feature private balconies or spacious terraces? Several apartment categories at master Mazeh include a furnished private outdoor space. These include the One-Bedroom with Balcony and the One-Bedroom with Balcony and Sofa Bed, both of which have balconies, as well as the One-Bedroom with Large Terrace & Sofa Bed.
  • How often is the full apartment cleaning and linen change performed at master Mazeh? Professional housekeeping and fresh linens are provided every seven days. Additional cleaning services are available upon request for a fee.

Food & Dining

  • Does master Mazeh serve breakfast on-site, and what style of breakfast is available? master Mazeh does not serve meals; all apartments are designed for a fully self-catering experience. Each apartment includes a fully equipped kitchen for guests to prepare their own food.
  • Are tea and coffee-making facilities, including an espresso machine, provided in every apartment at master Mazeh? Yes, every apartment contains an electric kettle and an espresso machine with complimentary coffee capsules and tea bags.
  • Is a lobby lounge or coffee bar available to guests at master Mazeh? Yes, a relaxed lobby lounge with complimentary coffee is available on site, offering a comfortable place to meet or work.
  • Does master Mazeh provide the kitchenware and cookware necessary for preparing meals in-apartment? Yes, complete sets of cookware, dishes, glassware, and utensils are stocked so guests can prepare and serve meals with ease.
  • Are a toaster and oven provided in the apartment kitchens at master Mazeh? Yes, the kitchenette includes a toaster and combination oven, enabling guests to cook or heat meals independently.
  • Where can guests shop for groceries near master Mazeh? Small supermarkets and convenience stores operate on nearby streets just a few minutes’ walk away.
  • Can food from local delivery apps be delivered directly to my apartment at master Mazeh? Guests can easily order meals through popular local apps such as Wolt and 10bis. To maintain building security, couriers deliver to the main entrance, where guests can collect their orders directly from the driver.

Check-in & Check-out

  • What are the standard check-in and check-out times at master Mazeh? Check-in is at 15:00 and check-out is by 11:00. Early check-in may be available subject to apartment readiness and availability.
  • How does the check-in process work? master Mazeh uses self-check-in: a PIN code is sent at 09:00 on arrival day via WhatsApp or email.
  • Is luggage storage available at master Mazeh for early arrivals or late departures? Yes, complimentary luggage storage is available before check-in or after check-out upon request.
  • What is the minimum age requirement for check-in at master Mazeh? The primary guest must be at least 18 years old to check in. Guests under the age of 30 are required to provide a refundable deposit of ₪1,500.
  • What form of payment is accepted for the damage deposit at master Mazeh, and when is it released? The security deposit is processed via credit card and will be released within seven business days.
  • Are guests required to present a photo ID and credit card upon check-in at master Mazeh? The online check-in process is completed in advance and requires ID verification, so there’s no need to show any identification or credit card upon arrival.
  • When exactly will I receive my access code for master Mazeh, and what should I do if it hasn’t arrived by check-in day? Your access code is sent at 09:00 on your arrival day via WhatsApp or email. If you have not received it by then, contact the guest support team immediately.
  • What is the procedure if my master Mazeh access code does not work when I arrive at the door? Please contact the Guest Relations Team immediately via WhatsApp (+97237386551) or email (easytravelagency@trustagent.space); a representative will verify your booking and assist.
  • How long does the master Mazeh online check-in process typically take to get verified? The online check-in verification takes only a few minutes once all required details are submitted.
  • How and when can I request an official invoice for my stay at master Mazeh? Official invoices are issued at the end of the stay. To ensure your document is accurate, please provide any specific billing details or company information to our team prior to your check-out date. Unfortunately, it is not possible to make changes to the invoice details after the invoice is issued.

Reservations & Policies

  • What are the cancellation and prepayment terms for reservations at master Mazeh? Cancellation and prepayment conditions vary by rate and apartment type; the policy shown during booking will apply.
  • Which credit cards are accepted for payment at master Mazeh? The property accepts American Express, Visa, Mastercard, Diners Club and Maestro cards; cash is not accepted.
  • Are pets permitted to stay at master Mazeh? No, unfortunately master Mazeh cannot accommodate pets.
  • Does master Mazeh require guests to sign a short-term rental agreement before arrival? Yes, as part of the online check-in process, a rental agreement must be signed electronically before your access codes can be issued.
  • Does the total price at master Mazeh include VAT? The confirmed price includes VAT for Israeli residents. For holders of an Israeli tourist visa, VAT is exempt and not included in the quoted rate.
  • What is the maximum occupancy for each apartment type at master Mazeh? While our standard One-Bedroom apartments host up to 2 guests, we offer larger options to accommodate more people. Our One-Bedroom with a sofa bed fits up to 4 guests, and our Two-Bedroom with a sofa bed can host up to 6 guests.

Attractions & Experiences

  • Do apartments at master Mazeh offer views of Tel Aviv’s UNESCO-listed White City architecture? Yes, many upper-floor apartments overlook the surrounding Bauhaus and eclectic “White City” buildings recognised by UNESCO.
  • Is the Bauhaus Center located within walking distance of master Mazeh? Yes, Tel Aviv’s Bauhaus Center on Dizengoff Street is about a 20-minute, 1.6-kilometre walk from the property.
  • Are Shenkin Street’s boutiques and cafés a short stroll from master Mazeh? Yes, Shenkin Street’s boutiques and cafés are located about 800 metres away, an easy ten-minute stroll from the apartments.
  • What are the best things to do in Tel Aviv when staying at master Mazeh? master Mazeh is in the heart of the action – moments from Rothschild Boulevard and the city’s best brunch spots. Whether guests want the beach, museums, markets or fine dining, everything is within easy reach.
