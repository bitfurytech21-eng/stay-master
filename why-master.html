Why master?

At master, experience the freedom of an extended stay with the comfort of home in our serviced apartments and aparthotels. Whether it be an open-ended business trip, a sabbatical or summer long holiday, our serviced apartments and aparthotels are designed for work and play, offering the freedom to live, work and recharge.

Our units offer the best of both worlds: the convenience of a full-equipped kitchen and dedicated workspaces, paired with the indulgence of hotel amenities, including toiletries, bedsheets and top-notch cleanliness.
