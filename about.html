Our Story

It all started 10 years ago. master set itself a mission to reshape the way people travel. We’ve blended the comfort of a hotel with thoughtfully designed, fully-equipped apartments in the world’s most exhilarating destinations. Whether you’re on an extended business trip, on sabbatical or with family on a staycation, experience the luxury of feeling right at home!

Our Vision

Welcome to the next generation of travel. More space, more freedom and more comfort – this is what guarantee the modern traveler. Our serviced apartments and aparthotels are meticulously tailored to meet the needs of the business traveler, families or those on an extended stay . From the moment you walk in, you’ll feel at ease and at home. Our exquisite apartments keep you connected and refreshed as you explore and conquer. Dive headfirst into the city’s culture, cuisine and lifestyle, and soon it will feel like home.

Our Values

  • Exceptional Service: At master, we’re all about convenience. Our personalized service is just a text (or Whatsapp) away! No need to download complicated apps. Our dedicated team of hospitality experts go the extra mile to provide you with genuinely sincere and personalized service that makes you feel truly one-of-a-kind. Whether you need an extra pillow, dry-cleaning services or a taxi, we’ve got your back covered. We’re there at the drop of a dime to ensure you’re every need is met.
  • Seamless Digital Experience: Welcome to the future of hospitality with our frictionless, seamless and stress-free communication processes. You’ll love the flexibility of our contactless self check-in: No more queues, no more forms – just a hassle-free arrival! Check in from your phone, any time, from anywhere. The process is super fast and smooth. Those of you who arrive outside of traditional check-in hours, no longer need to wait for staff to assist them. Need to contact us for any reason? Just grab your phone and give us a shout – we’re here 24/7 to satisfy your every demand!
  • Built for Living: Work, play, and stay your way: Whether it’s a weekend getaway or a year-long stay, our service apartments offer a dynamic space where you can both work hard and play hard. You’ll cherish the flexibility of our serviced apartments and aparthotels, where you can seamlessly transition between holding a meeting with colleagues and cooking up a gourmet meal to entertain them just afterwards. We empower you to achieve both your work goals all the while immersing yourself in a world of leisure.
