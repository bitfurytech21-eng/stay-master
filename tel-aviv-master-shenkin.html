master Shenkin

Hip and happening! Shenkin – the beating heart of Tel Aviv's hipster scene! This street is home to some of the city's coolest boutiques, bustling cafes, and funky shops, making it a must-visit destination for anyone looking to soak up the local culture.

The perfect place to wander, shop, grab a coffee or a bite to eat and people-watch. Near some of the city's top attractions such as the Carmel Market, Nahalat Binyamin and Rothschild Boulevard, there's no better place to experience Tel Aviv's vibrant energy.
