Getting here

  • To get from Ben Gurion Airport to 38 Shenkin Street in Tel Aviv, you have several transportation options. The most common methods are by taxi, private shuttle, or public transportation. Here are the steps for each option:
  • By taxi Taxis are readily available at Ben Gurion Airport 24/7. Follow the signs to the taxi stand.
  • Simply tell the taxi driver you want to go to “38 Shenkin Street in Tel Aviv.” Make sure the meter is turned on.
  • The journey to Shenkin Street from the airport should take approximately 20-30 minutes, depending on traffic.
  • By Private Shuttle You can pre-book a private shuttle service, such as a shared airport transfer or a private car, to take you from the airport to your destination.
  • Companies like SuperShuttle or various local providers offer these services.
  • Make sure to provide your flight details and address in advance when booking.
  • Public Transportation Take the train: There is a train station at Ben Gurion Airport connected to the airport terminal. Purchase a train ticket to Tel Aviv’s “HaHagana” or “Savidor Center” station.
  • From the train station, you can take a taxi or public transportation to Shenkin Street.
  • Take a bus: Ben Gurion Airport is served by several bus lines. Take Bus Line 445 or 475 to Tel Aviv’s central bus station (Arlozorov).
  • From there, you can transfer to other buses or use a taxi to reach Shenkin Street.
  • Check-in from 03:00 pm, check-out by 11:00 am.
  • Reception and support are remote — details are sent before arrival.
