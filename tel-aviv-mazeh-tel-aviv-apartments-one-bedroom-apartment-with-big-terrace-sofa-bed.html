Location

Nestled in a peaceful neighborhood that reflects the charm of early Tel Aviv, our location offers a mix of 1920s and 1930s eclectic and Bauhaus architecture. Despite its calm atmosphere, you’re just a short walk from Rothschild Boulevard’s vibrant cafes and nightlife. In 20 minutes, you can explore Carmel Market or relax at Aviv Beach, balancing tranquility and city life perfectly.
