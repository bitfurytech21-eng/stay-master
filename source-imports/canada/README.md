# Canada property source archive

- Captured: 2026-11-21
- Coverage: 15 representative public listing(s) across 15 city markets.
- Source: Vrbo public search results and listing pages.
- Files: `properties.json` contains listing source URLs, property identifiers, photo references, listing excerpts, amenities excerpts and a dated search snapshot; `properties.csv` is a compact index.

## Important usage notes

These are third-party listings, not Stay Master-owned or managed inventory. No booking, availability, or commercial relationship is implied. Sample prices are USD snapshots returned for two adults for 15–18 December 2026 and can change; do not publish them as Stay Master rates. Image URLs are externally hosted by Vrbo/Expedia; do not copy or republish images without confirming rights and platform terms. Verify all property details, legal short-term-rental requirements and permissions before any public use. This is a representative reference collection, not an exhaustive Canadian catalogue.
