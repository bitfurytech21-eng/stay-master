Mirabell Salzburg

When visiting Salzburg, the majestic and picturesque birthplace of Mozart, nestled in the embrace of the Austrian Alps, our spacious serviced apartments offer the perfect solution, whether you're on business or on extended vacation.

Embraced by the Baroque splendor of Mirabell Palace & Gardens and adjacent to the Congress House of Salzburg, our prime location offers convenient access to the city's renowned attractions. A mere 6-minute walk separates you from the iconic birthplace of Mozart, where his musical genius still resonates in opulent concert halls and intimate venues. A leisurely 10-minute stroll leads you to the UNESCO-listed Old Town, where pastel-colored buildings, ornate facades and pedestrian streets transport you to a bygone era. We are also just a stone's throw away from the gorgeous Salzach River – ramble along its shores as majestic mountains provide a dramatic backdrop to the city's architectural wonders. master Mirabell serviced apartments allow Salzburg to cast the spell of its magical atmosphere upon you, inviting you to discover a captivating blend of history, culture and natural allure.
