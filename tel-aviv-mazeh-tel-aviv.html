Mazeh Tel Aviv

master Mazeh Tel Aviv Serviced Apartments is nestled in a peaceful neighborhood that embodies the charm and character of early Tel Aviv. The street is lined with historic buildings from the 1920s and 1930s, showcasing a mix of eclectic and Bauhaus architectural styles that reflect the city's beginnings. Recently restored, many of these architectural gems have become a must-see for design enthusiasts and a key attraction for visitors seeking a quieter side of the bustling city.

Despite its serene atmosphere, our location places you at the heart of Tel Aviv's cultural pulse. A short walk will take you to the iconic Rothschild Boulevard, known for its vibrant cafes, nightlife, and green spaces. In just 20 minutes, you can find yourself exploring the lively Carmel Market or relaxing at Aviv Beach, providing the perfect balance of tranquility and city life.
