Mazeh Tel Aviv

master Mazeh Tel Aviv Serviced Apartments is nestled in a peaceful neighborhood that embodies the charm and character of early Tel Aviv. The street is lined with historic buildings from the 1920s and 1930s, showcasing a mix of eclectic and Bauhaus architectural styles that reflect the city's beginnings. Recently restored, many of these architectural gems have become a must-see for design enthusiasts and a key attraction for visitors seeking a quieter side of the bustling city.

Despite its serene atmosphere, our location places you at the heart of Tel Aviv's cultural pulse. A short walk will take you to the iconic Rothschild Boulevard, known for its vibrant cafes, nightlife, and green spaces. In just 20 minutes, you can find yourself exploring the lively Carmel Market or relaxing at Aviv Beach, providing the perfect balance of tranquility and city life.

54 apartments

The Apartments

Our Serviced Apartments feature 54 spacious and modern serviced apartments, ranging from 42 to 60 square meters, ideal for accommodating 4 to 6 guests. Designed with comfort and convenience in mind, these apartments are perfect whether you're planning a short getaway or an extended stay.

Each apartment is equipped with a fully functional kitchenette, featuring a refrigerator, stovetop, microwave-oven combo, and an espresso machine to start your day right. Complimentary high-speed internet and built-in Chromecast ensure that you stay connected and entertained throughout your visit. We prioritize your well-being by providing fresh towels, branded bath products, and essential in-room amenities like a washer/dryer, iron, ironing board, and a safe for your valuables. Our dedicated remote staff is on hand to assist with any needs, ensuring your experience exceeds expectations. At master Mazeh Tel Aviv Serviced Apartments, you'll enjoy the comfort of a home-like environment paired with the services of a hotel, allowing you to relax and unwind after a day of exploring the dynamic city at your doorstep.
