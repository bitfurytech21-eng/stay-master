You’re Welcome

An open-ended business trip, a sabbatical, or a summer-long family holiday. There are many reasons to stay with us.

Business trips

Host a meeting in your living room or jump on a Zoom call using our free blazing-fast Wi-Fi. Working from home just got a completely new meaning. Special corporate rates are available.

Long Stays

No more cramped hotel rooms. Enjoy spacious living areas, plenty of room to unroll your yoga mat, and full kitchens so you don’t have to eat out every night. Free-to-use laundry facilities are available in our properties. Enjoy special rates for long stays.

Leisure stays

See the city through the eyes of a local. Stay in the best location, pick up some local ingredients at the market, and get a Flat White where the locals get it. It’s your place now. Livin’ la vida local.

Families

For families on a city break or a staycation. Instead of getting a few hotel rooms, a multi-bedroom apartment is the perfect base for exploring (and keeping your sanity). Children of all ages are welcome.
