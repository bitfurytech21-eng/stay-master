General Information

  • Is master Shenkin more like staying in a hotel or renting a private apartment in Tel Aviv? master Shenkin offers Serviced Apartments that combine private living spaces with hotel-style services, including virtual guest support, housekeeping, and laundry facilities.
  • What type of traveller is master Shenkin best suited for (family, business, or solo)? master Shenkin suits business travellers, couples, solo guests, and families seeking spacious serviced apartments with kitchen facilities in central Tel Aviv.
  • Is master Shenkin set up for business travellers on a corporate stay in Tel Aviv? Yes. Apartments include a laptop workspace, high-speed Wi-Fi, and a central Tel Aviv location suitable for business stays.
  • Is master Shenkin suitable for a long-term stay of several weeks or more? Yes. Fully equipped kitchens, washer-dryers, weekly housekeeping, and apartment-style layouts make it comfortable for extended stays.
  • Is there a workspace or co-working area at master Shenkin for remote workers? Apartments include a laptop workspace and reliable high-speed internet suitable for remote work. A dedicated co-working area is not listed among the property facilities.
  • Does master Shenkin provide complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi throughout all apartments and public areas? Yes, complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi is available in every apartment and all common areas for unlimited use.
  • Does master Shenkin feature a 24-hour fitness centre for guests? Yes, master Shenkin has a small yet fully equipped gym providing everything you need for a complete, convenient workout at any hour.
  • Does master Shenkin allow guests free access to its panoramic rooftop terrace? Yes, guests of master Shenkin may freely enjoy the rooftop terrace’s panoramic city and sea views throughout the day.
  • Does master Shenkin offer on-site self-parking, and what is the daily fee? Yes, secure on-site self-parking is offered for ₪60 per day (not including VAT), with advance reservation required.
  • Is master Shenkin a 100% smoke-free building? Yes, the entire building is smoke-free, and smoking is prohibited inside all apartments and indoor areas.
  • Does master Shenkin offer complimentary luggage storage before check-in or after check-out? Yes, master Shenkin offers complimentary luggage storage before check-in and after check-out; service is available daily from 09:00 to 17:00.

Location & Transportation

  • What is the address of master Shenkin? The building is located at 38 Shenkin Street in central Tel Aviv’s lively White City district.
  • How long does it take to walk from master Shenkin to Rothschild Boulevard? Rothschild Boulevard is about a four-minute walk (roughly 0.4 km) from the property entrance.
  • Is master Shenkin within walking distance of the Carmel Market and Nahalat Binyamin Street Fair? Yes, both the Carmel Market and the Nahalat Binyamin Street Fair are only a few minutes’ stroll from the entrance.
  • How far is master Shenkin from Jerusalem Beach and the Mediterranean promenade? Jerusalem Beach and the Mediterranean promenade are approximately 1 km, about a 10-minute walk, from master Shenkin.
  • Does master Shenkin arrange private airport transfers from Ben Gurion Airport? No, master Shenkin does not arrange private airport transfers; we recommend ordering a licensed Gett taxi for the most reliable service from Ben Gurion Airport.
  • What are the nearest public transport stations to master Shenkin? Hashalom Train Station is the nearest major stop, reachable from the property by a short taxi or bus ride.
  • What is the typical taxi journey time from master Shenkin to Ben Gurion Airport? A metered taxi usually reaches the airport in roughly 20–35 minutes, depending on traffic conditions.

Rooms & Amenities

  • Do all apartments at master Shenkin include a fully equipped kitchen with oven, stovetop, microwave and dishwasher? Yes, every apartment has a fully equipped kitchen featuring an oven, stovetop, microwave, dishwasher and full cookware.
  • Does every apartment at master Shenkin have a Smart TV with Netflix? Yes, each apartment has a Smart TV with built-in Netflix streaming for effortless entertainment.
  • Which sheets and bedding does master Shenkin provide? Beds are dressed with Egyptian-cotton sheets and premium bedding for superior comfort.
  • Is a washer–dryer available in each apartment at master Shenkin? Yes, every apartment contains its own washer and dryer for convenient in-suite laundry.
  • Are laptop-size safes installed in every apartment at master Shenkin? Yes, laptop-compatible safes are installed in all apartments for secure storage of valuables.
  • Does master Shenkin offer complimentary cribs or infant beds on request? Yes, cribs or infant beds are available free of charge when requested in advance.
  • How often is the full apartment cleaning and linen change performed at master Shenkin? At master Shenkin, complimentary full housekeeping is provided weekly for stays of seven nights or longer. Additional or more-frequent cleans can be arranged on request for an additional fee.

Food & Dining

  • Do the kitchens at master Shenkin include a full-size refrigerator and separate dining area? Yes, all kitchens feature a full-size refrigerator and a separate dining area for comfortable in-room meals.
  • Does master Shenkin provide complimentary tea, coffee and Nespresso facilities on arrival? Yes, complimentary tea, regular coffee and Nespresso capsules are provided in the apartment on arrival.
  • Does master Shenkin have an on-site bar or restaurant service? No, there is no on-site bar or restaurant; apartments are fully self-catered.
  • May guests of master Shenkin bring their own food and beverages to enjoy on the rooftop terrace? Yes, guests are welcome to take their own food and drinks to the rooftop terrace.
  • Are highchairs available for infants and toddlers dining in the apartments at master Shenkin? Yes, highchairs are available on request for infants and toddlers.
  • Is food delivery available to guests staying at master Shenkin? Guests can easily order meals through popular local apps such as Wolt and 10bis. To maintain building security, couriers deliver to the main entrance, where guests can collect their orders directly from the driver.

Check-in & Check-out

  • What are the standard check-in and check-out times at master Shenkin? Check-in starts at 15:00 and check-out is required by 11:00.
  • Is early check-in available at master Shenkin? Yes, early check-in can be arranged subject to availability and an additional fee.
  • Can guests arrange late check-out at master Shenkin, and what charges apply? Yes, late check-out at master Shenkin can be pre-arranged, subject to availability and any applicable additional fees.
  • What is the minimum age required to check in at master Shenkin? Guests must be at least 25 years old to check in unless accompanied by a parent or legal guardian. Please note that for primary guests under the age of 30, a mandatory security deposit of NIS 1,500 is required.
  • What identification and credit card are required at check-in for master Shenkin? Because check-in is completed online in advance with ID verification, you will not need to present identification or a credit card upon arrival.
  • Are quiet hours enforced at master Shenkin? Yes, quiet hours are strictly enforced nightly from 21:00 until 08:00.
  • Do guests need to complete online pre-registration before arrival? Yes, before arrival at master Shenkin you must complete the online check-in process; access codes are issued once this step is completed.
  • How do guests check in at master Shenkin? master Shenkin operates self-check-in: your apartment PIN code is sent via WhatsApp and email by 09:00 on arrival day, enabling fully contactless entry and departure.
  • When exactly will I receive my access code for master Shenkin, and what should I do if it hasn’t arrived by check-in day? The property operates with a self-check-in system. Your unique apartment PIN code will be sent to you via WhatsApp and email at 9:00 AM on your arrival day. To ensure you receive your code on time, please confirm that your reservation is paid in full and your online registration is complete.
  • Will I be required to send a copy of my ID or passport by email or during online registration before getting the master Shenkin access code? Yes. Before arrival, guests must complete online registration through a secure link, and the property states that a copy of the passport is requested after booking before access is issued.
  • What is the procedure if my master Shenkin access code does not work when I arrive at the door? Please contact the Guest Relations Team immediately via WhatsApp (+97237386551) or email (easytravelagency@trustagent.space). A representative will verify your booking and assist.

Reservations & Policies

  • What is the cancellation policy for reservations at master Shenkin? Cancellation and prepayment terms depend on the chosen rate; the conditions displayed during booking apply to your reservation.
  • Is a damage deposit required at master Shenkin? Guests under 30 years old must provide a ₪1,500 refundable damage deposit, pre-authorised on their credit card before arrival.
  • Does master Shenkin charge VAT for foreign tourists or offer exemptions? An 18% Israeli VAT applies to Israeli citizens, including dual nationals; foreign tourists (non-Israeli passport holders who are not permanent residents) are exempt.
  • Are dogs permitted at master Shenkin, and are there additional fees? Yes, dogs are welcome for an additional fee of NIS 80 (per night, not including VAT); they must be trained, never left unattended, kept off sofas and beds, and any damage will be charged to the guest.
  • What payment methods are accepted by master Shenkin for accommodation and deposits? All stays must be pre-paid using the secure payment link provided. We exclusively accept card payments via this link and are unable to accept cash at the property.
  • Are parties or events allowed at master Shenkin? No, parties or events are not permitted anywhere on the premises.
  • What is the maximum occupancy for each apartment type at master Shenkin? One-Bedroom Apartments accommodate up to 4 guests, the Two-Bedroom Apartment up to 6, Three-Bedroom Apartments up to 8, the Four-Bedroom Penthouse up to 9, and the Four-Bedroom Apartment with Balcony and Five-Bedroom duplex garden up to 10 guests.

  • How long does it take to walk from master Shenkin to Gordon Beach on the Tel Aviv Promenade? Gordon Beach lies about 2 km away, a comfortable 25-minute walk from the property.
  • What nightlife spots are near master Shenkin on Shenkin Street? Shenkin Street features trendy cafés and stylish boutiques, and it is an easy walk to Tel Aviv’s many bars and clubs.
