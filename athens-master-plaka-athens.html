Plaka Athens

Nestled on Apollonos 34 in the heart of Plaka, master Plaka Athens offers a unique blend of modern living within one of Athens's most historic and charming neighborhoods. Located just below the iconic Acropolis Hill, Plaka invites you to experience the essence of old Athens, with narrow cobblestone streets, neoclassical buildings, and vibrant local life.

Plaka is renowned for its nostalgic atmosphere, blending small cafes, traditional tavernas, quaint souvenir shops, and ancient ruins around every corner. While it is lively and bustling with visitors, it retains an intimate and authentic charm that sets it apart from the rest of the city center. Here you can explore the area's rich history or enjoy a leisurely walk through its pedestrian-friendly streets. Plaka invites you to discover, unwind, and explore at your own pace. At master Plaka Athens, we provide 14 thoughtfully designed apartments, tailored to meet the needs of modern travelers. Whether you're visiting for business or leisure, for a short or extended stay, our apartments are crafted to offer the perfect balance of comfort and convenience. For added ease, the property is equipped with an elevator, ensuring smooth access to all apartment levels. Guests also have access to a communal area, an inviting space to work, relax, or connect with others. No matter your purpose—whether for business meetings or a cultural escape—master Plaka Athens is your perfect home base, offering an authentic stay in the heart of the city.

The Apartments

The apartments at master Plaka Athens are designed to provide a harmonious blend of style and functionality, offering a serene escape within the vibrant surroundings of the city. Each of the 14 apartments reflects a modern minimalist aesthetic, with clean lines, neutral tones, and thoughtfully curated furnishings to create a sense of calm.

Equipped with fully-functional kitchens, coffee-making facilities, high-speed Wi-Fi, and flat-screen TVs, the apartments are well-suited for both short-term stays and extended visits. Whether you're traveling for business or leisure, these apartments provide all the essentials you need, ensuring a seamless and comfortable stay in the heart of Athens.
