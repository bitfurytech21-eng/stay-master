Plaka Athens

Nestled on Apollonos 34 in the heart of Plaka, master Plaka Athens offers a unique blend of modern living within one of Athens's most historic and charming neighborhoods. Located just below the iconic Acropolis Hill, Plaka invites you to experience the essence of old Athens, with narrow cobblestone streets, neoclassical buildings, and vibrant local life.

Plaka is renowned for its nostalgic atmosphere, blending small cafes, traditional tavernas, quaint souvenir shops, and ancient ruins around every corner. While it is lively and bustling with visitors, it retains an intimate and authentic charm that sets it apart from the rest of the city center. Here you can explore the area's rich history or enjoy a leisurely walk through its pedestrian-friendly streets. Plaka invites you to discover, unwind, and explore at your own pace. At master Plaka Athens, we provide 14 thoughtfully designed apartments, tailored to meet the needs of modern travelers. Whether you're visiting for business or leisure, for a short or extended stay, our apartments are crafted to offer the perfect balance of comfort and convenience. For added ease, the property is equipped with an elevator, ensuring smooth access to all apartment levels. Guests also have access to a communal area, an inviting space to work, relax, or connect with others. No matter your purpose—whether for business meetings or a cultural escape—master Plaka Athens is your perfect home base, offering an authentic stay in the heart of the city.

The neighbourhood

Located just below the Acropolis Hill, Plaka is one of the most picturesque neighborhoods in Athens, offering a delightful mix of history, culture, and charm. Its narrow streets, neoclassical buildings, and ancient ruins at every turn create an atmosphere that feels like stepping back in time.

Known for its lively cafes, traditional tavernas, and unique souvenir shops, Plaka exudes the nostalgic spirit of old Athens, setting it apart from the rest of the bustling city center. While popular with visitors, it retains an authentic charm, making it the perfect place to enjoy a leisurely stroll through its pedestrian-friendly streets.

At master Plaka Athens, you are perfectly positioned to explore this vibrant neighborhood, with iconic landmarks, cultural sites, and local flavors just steps from your door.

Getting here

  • Here’s how to get to Apollonos 34 in Athens from different directions:
  • From Athens International Airport (ATH) 1. Metro: – Take the Metro Line 3 (the blue line) towards Agia Marina. – Transfer at Monastiraki Station to Line 1 (the green line) and get off at the next stop, Omonia. – From Omonia, you can walk or take a short taxi ride to Apollonos 34. 2. Taxi: – A taxi ride takes about 30-40 minutes and costs around €40-60, depending on traffic. 3. Bus: – Take the X95 bus to Syntagma Square. From there, it’s about a 10-minute walk to Apollonos 34.
  • From Athens City Center (Syntagma Square) 1. Walk: – It’s about a 10-minute walk. Head southeast towards Apollonos Street. 2.Taxi: – A taxi will take about 5 minutes. 3. Metro: – You can also take the Metro from Syntagma Station to Monastiraki Station on Line 1.
  • From Piraeus Port 1.Metro: – Take Metro Line 1 (the green line) towards Kifisia and transfer at Monastiraki Station to Line 3 to get closer if needed. 2. Taxi: – A taxi will take about 30 minutes, depending on traffic.
  • Check-in from 03:00 pm, check-out by 11:00 am.
  • Reception and support are remote — details are sent before arrival.
