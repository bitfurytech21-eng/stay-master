Getting Here

  • To get to Via in Arcione 98, 00187 Roma from Ciampino Airport (CIA) and Fiumicino Airport (FCO), here are your options:
  • From Ciampino Airport (CIA): Taxi: Taxis are available outside the airport. It takes about 30-40 minutes, and the fare to central Rome is around €30-€40.
  • Shuttle Bus + Metro: Take a shuttle bus (e.g., Terravision or SIT bus) from Ciampino Airport to Roma Termini (the main train station). The bus ride takes about 40 minutes and costs €5-€7. From Termini, take Metro Line A (direction Battistini) and get off at Barberini station. From there, it’s a 5-minute walk to Via in Arcione.
  • Train + Bus: Take a bus from Ciampino Airport to the Ciampino railway station (about 10 minutes). From there, take a regional train to Roma Termini (takes 15 minutes). Then follow the same directions from Termini as above.
  • From Fiumicino Airport (FCO): Taxi: A taxi ride takes around 40-50 minutes, and the fixed fare to central Rome is €48.
  • Leonardo Express Train + Metro: Take the Leonardo Express from Fiumicino to Roma Termini. The train runs every 15 minutes, and the journey takes 32 minutes. Tickets cost €14. From Termini, take Metro Line A (direction Battistini) and get off at Barberini station.
  • Bus + Metro: Take a shuttle bus (Terravision, SIT bus) from Fiumicino Airport to Roma Termini (about 45-60 minutes, costs €6-€8). From Termini, take Metro Line A to Barberini. Via in Arcione is centrally located near the Trevi Fountain, so it’s well-connected by public transport.
